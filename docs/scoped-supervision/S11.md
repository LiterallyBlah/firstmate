# S11: Strong-mode capability gate and the conservative cooperative path

Disabled implementation for independent R11 review, based on landed S07–S10 (`origin/main` `d37c28f4`, PR #158).
It installs nothing and registers no RPC, hook, timer or route. It activates no scope, executor or capability,
sends nothing and writes nothing to the supervision journal.

## What D6 selected, and what that means here

D6 (`data/fm-ss-d6`) is answered with option 2, the **conservative rollout amendment**. U1–U3 are not delivered
upstream and are not assumed:

- U1: authenticated caller channels and receiver fences.
- U2: durable subscription and heartbeat transfer.
- U3: conditional archive.

The slice contract's strong-mode outcome ("consume U1/U2/U3") is therefore **deferred, not waived or passed**. In
its place, S11 delivers three things:

1. **Explicit dependencies for drained cooperative scopes** (`server/supervision-cooperative.ts`). This is a pure
   read, `cooperativeReadiness(status, scopeId)`. It names each earlier slice's condition separately and holds any
   scope that does not meet every one.
2. **The S00 capability probes, kept** (`server/supervision-capabilities.ts`). Strong mode can be enabled later only
   with a public contract, a supported installed version and live receiver evidence. Today every strong capability
   is refused, and nothing falls back silently.
3. **The R10 round-3 executor-approval conditions**, carried forward:
   - realpath, E1, E2 and E3 are fixed in code;
   - the live-daemon reader check has been run;
   - N7 stays at the advisory gate.

### Limits, stated plainly

These limits are also the `COOPERATIVE_LIMITS` that every report returns:

- **Cooperative only.** The plugin refuses stale commands at its own boundary. A full-access session can bypass it
  through native tools, the CLI or a shell.
- **No strong isolation.** There is no authenticated caller channel, no receiver fence and no raw-capability
  confinement.
- **No automatic failover or takeover.** A supervisor is replaced only manually, after its exact predecessor is shown
  stopped. This is S04's existing `prepareReplacement`, unchanged.
- **No durable callback or heartbeat transfer.** A legacy worker's native finish notification still goes to its
  original creator. It is drained or held, and the worker is never re-prompted to rearm.
- **No conditional archive.** Archive needs a complete persisted inventory (`archiveChildren`), and the last-check race
  remains. Incomplete ancestry refuses.
- **Reservations are advisory.** No receiver checks a fence, so an advisory lock is never presented as enforced.

A ready scope is cooperative, nothing more. `cooperativeRollout` never claims fleet-wide relief:
`fleetWideRelief: false`. A held scope still delivers to General, and the full source, owner and timer census
belongs to S12.

## Cooperative readiness (`cooperativeReadiness`, `cooperativeRollout`)

Each dependency is reported with its slice and every reason it is unmet. Only a scope with all of them satisfied is
`ready`.

| Dependency | Slice | Holds the scope when |
|---|---|---|
| `journal` | S01 | The journal is not `current`, or its sequence has reached a band: S08 at 128, S10 at 160, S04/S09 at 192. Past a band, the observations it stops may be stale, so the result is a `lowerBound` |
| `scope` | S03 | The scope is unregistered, still `legacy`, or `held`, `transferring` or `retired` |
| `binding` | S04 | A supervisor epoch is `prepared` or `suspect`: its owner is not shown current, and replacement is manual |
| `cutover` | S07 | A plan's backend is not scoped/active (or retired). The plan's latest migration is `prepared` or `barrier`, or was `aborted` back to legacy |
| `deliveries` | S07 | A legacy delivery is an unresolved `hold`. Its removal is `pending` or `mismatch`. A transferred fact has no recorded acknowledgement from the current owner (S07's own "awaits its owner's result") |
| `effects` | S04 | An effect is `dispatching`, `accepted` or `uncertain`. It keeps its reservation and is never retried |
| `workers` | S07 | An attempt's native callback owner is `unknown`. An imported legacy attempt is still `starting`, `running` or `unresolved`, so its callback still goes to its original owner. A plan node is `unresolved` |
| `reservations` | S10 | A reservation is `suspect`: its lease expired without terminal evidence |
| `dependencies` | S09 | A cross-plan dependency resolution is `held`: missing, ambiguous, removed or cross-backend |
| `board-sends` | S08 | A board message is `reserved` or `uncertain` |
| `holds` | S03/S09 | An operation hold is `held`, for example after a project stop |

`reserved` effects and `granted` reservations do not hold a scope. They are ordinary admitted work in the
cooperative pilot. Only uncertainty, an unaccounted owner or an unfinished cutover hold it.

### Journal bands

S11 writes nothing, so it consumes no budget in any band:

- S08 stops at 128.
- S10 admits up to 160 and closes up to 192.
- S04/S09 stop at 192.
- S07's release reservation is untouched.

`JOURNAL_BANDS` reads those ceilings from the owning constants (`SOURCE_LIMITS.observationCeiling`,
`MAINTENANCE_ADMISSION_CEILING`, `SUPERVISION_INDEX_LIMITS.admissions`), so they cannot drift.

## Strong capabilities (`evaluateStrongCapabilities`, `requireStrong`)

| Capability | Upstream | S00 rows that must be `source-supported` |
|---|---|---|
| `authenticated-caller` | U1 | `authenticated-admission` |
| `receiver-fence` | U1 | `authenticated-admission`, `send-receipt` |
| `durable-subscriptions` | U2 | `finish-transfer` |
| `heartbeat-transfer` | U2 | `heartbeat-owner` |
| `conditional-archive` | U3 | `conditional-archive` |

A capability is `enabled` only when all of these hold:

- Its flag is set. `FIRSTMATE_STRONG_CAPABILITIES` is a comma list, empty by default, and nothing reads it yet.
- The installed version maps to an S00 column: 0.11 floor, 0.12 proposal or 0.13 disk. Any other version is
  unqualified; nothing is borrowed from a newer document.
- Every S00 row it rests on is `source-supported` in that column.
- A `StrongQualification` covers the installed version. That record must have:
  - a public contract (package, semver and signature);
  - a supported range;
  - live receiver evidence: at least a receiver canary and a raw-tool/CLI/shell bypass analysis;
  - `qualification: "live-receiver"`. A synthetic fixture is rejected by the schema.

`S00_STRONG_ROWS` is a copy of the frozen S00 table, and a test holds it equal to
`test/fixtures/supervision-s00/examples.json`. Every strong row is `unsupported` in every column, and
`SHIPPED_QUALIFICATIONS` is empty. So on the installed 0.13.0, as on 0.11 and 0.12, every requested capability is
`refused`.

`requireStrong` then throws `UNSUPPORTED`. There is no cooperative fallback for that call ("nothing falls back to
cooperative mode").

The report's claims are explicit:

- **Automatic failover and takeover:** true only with all four U1/U2 capabilities.
- **`fullAccessConfinement`:** always `false`. Confinement of full-access sessions needs OS or credential isolation,
  which this plugin cannot provide.

Malformed qualification records and unknown flags are reported in `problems` and never used.

`server/supervision-receivers.ts` holds the U1–U3 adapter shapes from the plan's contract sketches, with strict
schemas for receipts.

- `bindStrongReceivers` refuses a receiver offered for any capability that is not enabled, rather than ignoring it.
- `readAdmission` reads an answer outside the U1 contract, or for another effect, as `uncertain`. It is never
  admitted.
- `refuseStaleAdmission` turns a receiver's `stale-fence` rejection into `STALE`.

No adapter is implemented, and no private protocol message stands in for one.

## Executor-approval conditions (R10 round 3)

`EXECUTOR_CONDITIONS` and `executorApproval` state what approving a maintenance executor needs. No executor ships,
and S10's advisory pass still refuses without one.

| Condition | Status in this slice |
|---|---|
| **realpath** | **Code.** `resolveCleanupAliases(candidates, retained, realpath)`: <ul><li>Keeps each retained path under its literal and its resolved name.</li><li>A missing retained path keeps its literal name. Any other resolution failure refuses the pass (`RetainedPathError`).</li><li>Gives each candidate its `realPath`. `planCleanup` holds both names to the same selector and retained bounds.</li><li>Drops and counts a candidate that cannot be resolved.</li></ul> Tested with a real junction on Windows (a symlink on Linux): the directory holding a worktree reported through an alias, and a candidate aliasing out of its selector, are both excluded |
| **E1** | **Code.** `within` treats a parent ending in `/` (`/`, `c:/`) as a root that contains every path on it. A session at `/` or `C:\` now protects its whole root. Another drive's root protects nothing there |
| **E2** | **Code.** `\\?\C:\…` and `\\.\C:\…` are read as `C:\…`. These refuse the pass: <ul><li>other `\\?\` and `\\.\` forms (UNC, `Volume{…}`, devices);</li><li>a `?` segment;</li><li>a drive-shaped segment mid-path.</li></ul> |
| **E3** | **Code.** `JobInputSchema.retained` accepts an identity (no separator, no `~`, no drive prefix) or an absolute path that canonicalizes. `~/keep`, `C:cache\keep`, `C:keep`, `cache/keep` and `/cache/../keep` are refused when the job is defined (`retainedEntryValid`) |
| **live-readers** | **Evidence.** See below. `executorApproval` accepts only `ReaderEvidence` whose `daemonVersion` equals the installed version and which shows both readers complete. A later Paseo version needs a fresh run |
| **N7** | **Gate.** Duplicate-cleaner detection without a declared timer link stays a decision at the advisory-activation gate. It needs the captain's explicit acknowledgement |
| **receiver** | **Gate.** An approved executor would still be cooperative. Reservations are advisory and claim no exclusion. This needs the captain's explicit acknowledgement |

### Live-daemon readers on the installed version

`docs/scoped-supervision/s11-readers/run.mjs` does the following:

- Starts a **throwaway** daemon from the installed Paseo with its own home and a loopback port. That home lives under
  ignored `.local/`; the run is not desktop-managed and has relay off.
- Creates one paused, far-future schedule (`0 0 1 1 *`, `--max-runs 1`). It never fires: the daemon is stopped and its
  home deleted at the end of the run.
- Installs a minimal probe plugin whose server half is the **unmodified** `server/archive-inventory.ts` and
  `server/daemon-session.ts`. Their SHA-256s are recorded.
- Has that plugin call `retainedWorkspaces()` and `nativeSchedules()` once over its own plugin IPC channel, and records
  what they returned.

No model, provider session or agent was started.

Result (`s11-readers/result.json`):

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Installed Paseo | 0.13.0 (Windows 11, `win32` 10.0.26200) |
| Plugin | `running` |
| `retainedWorkspaces()` | Complete (`workspaces: []`, `paths: []`). The paginated history request was accepted, its response parsed, and the final-page checks passed. The plugin channel stayed open |
| `nativeSchedules()` | `complete: true`, one row: cron `0 0 1 1 *` in UTC, a `new-agent` target with its `cwd`, `paused`. It parsed through `NativeScheduleSchema` |
| Verdict | Both readers complete |

**Limit.** The history listing was empty, because creating a session would start a provider. Multi-page pagination
and the missing-`workspaceId`/`cwd` refusal are covered only by S10's fixture tests, not by this live run. Linux was
not run live.

## Acceptance scenarios (S11's conservative part)

Each E assertion stays explicitly **unqualified** for the disabled strong capabilities. What S11 shows is the
H/refusal part.

| Scenario | Evidence |
|---|---|
| A01 | The cooperative limit names file-actor impersonation as outside the guarantee. `authenticated-caller` is refused on every inspected version, and `requireStrong` throws `UNSUPPORTED` |
| A04 | A `suspect` reservation holds the scope. `receiver-fence` is refused. `readAdmission`/`refuseStaleAdmission` give the shape a future receiver must satisfy: a delayed old fence is `STALE`, a foreign or malformed answer `uncertain`. No receiver evidence exists, so E stays unqualified |
| A05 | An operation hold (project stop) holds its scope. A dispatching, accepted or uncertain effect holds only its own scope; the unrelated scope stays ready |
| A06 | The S08 band at 128 makes readiness a lower bound and not ready. Board sends with unknown outcomes hold |
| A07 | S07 cutover, on the actual legacy adapters (`supervision-migrate.test.ts`): <ul><li>**L1:** not ready while the transferred fact awaits its owner; ready once acknowledged.</li><li>**L5:** ready after the opaque watch hold is explicitly drained to its legacy owner.</li><li>**Release:** an aborted cutover is held on `scope` and `cutover`.</li></ul> |
| A10 | Uncertain effects and `unresolved` nodes hold. There is no automatic takeover: failover/takeover claims are false |
| A14 | **B5/M1:** an imported legacy worker that is still running holds `workers`. Its callback still goes to General, and its owner is unknown. `conditional-archive` is refused; archive remains the complete-inventory refusal |
| A18 | Every strong capability is supported or refused, explicitly. An uninspected or unknown version refuses even with a qualification. A synthetic qualification is rejected. No private API is invented |

## Rollout and rollback

- **Rollout.** Nothing is activated. A later conservative rollout (S12) may use `cooperativeRollout` to list
  reconciled scopes. Its own gates still apply: R12/R12O, the exact installation and routing authority, and the
  observation window. Enabling a strong capability later needs, in order:
  1. U1–U3 delivered;
  2. a fresh S00 probe;
  3. a `StrongQualification` with live receiver evidence;
  4. its flag;
  5. R11-equivalent review.
- **Rollback.** There is nothing to roll back: S11 writes no state. A refused strong capability never downgrades to
  cooperative mode on its own. The caller holds the effect and keeps its epoch and receipts.

`paseo-plugin.json` stays at `>=0.11.0`: no strong capability is enabled, so no requirement is raised.
`server/supervisor-sessions.ts`, `server/supervision-effects.ts` and `server/remote-crew.ts` are unchanged:

- manual replacement only;
- the existing U1/U3 refusal in `SupervisionGateway.capabilities()`;
- S08's scoped remote-mutation refusal.

## Overlap

- **New files:** `server/supervision-capabilities.ts`, `server/supervision-receivers.ts`,
  `server/supervision-cooperative.ts`, and their tests.
- **`server/supervision-jobs.ts`:**
  - the root, long-path and drive-segment rules (E1/E2);
  - `retainedEntryValid` on `JobInputSchema.retained` (E3);
  - `resolveCleanupAliases`;
  - the optional `MaintenanceCandidate.realPath`, checked alongside `path`.
    Both names must lie inside a selector, compared exactly. A resolved path whose case differs from a selector typed in
    another case is excluded: this over-excludes, which is safe.
    Selectors must therefore name canonical (resolved) directories. A selector written through an alias, such as a
    Windows 8.3 short temp path, excludes everything rather than widening (tested).
- **`server/archive-inventory.ts`:** unchanged. The live check ran the committed file as it is.
- **`server/supervision-migrate.test.ts`:** readiness assertions in L1, L5, B5/M1 and release.

No reducer, store row, journal write, gate, workflow, Needs-you, mate or watch behaviour changes.
