# Generated sea, sky and gull assets

Standalone art for hull C. Nothing is wired into the plugin. `environment.json` is the asset manifest;
it is independent of the existing `deck.json` contract. All paths in it are relative to this folder.

| Asset | Logical size | Use | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `sea/water-tile.png` | 256 x 128 | Opaque repeating sea; scroll in whole pixels | usable |
| `sea/foam-strip.png` | 256 x 16 | Transparent, horizontally repeating hull-waterline overlay | usable-with-cleanup |
| `sky/{dawn,morning,midday,afternoon,dusk,night}.png` | 512 x 320 each | Six opaque, registered backgrounds | usable |
| `sky/stars.png` | 512 x 320 | Transparent night overlay | usable |
| `gull/{up,mid,down}.png` | 32 x 32 each | Three right-facing flap poses | usable-with-cleanup |
| `gull/flap-strip.png` | 96 x 32 | The same frames, left to right | usable-with-cleanup |

Use nearest-neighbour sampling and the same logical pixel scale as the 426 x 161 hull and 32 px crew.
The gull's visible silhouette is about 20-23 px wide. The PNGs have binary alpha, and transparent pixels
have zero RGB. The shared palette contains the **original 48 deck colours unchanged plus 16 muted
environment colours**. Daylight blues and twilight colours were absent from the hull palette. The
manifest records all 64 colours; water uses eight, foam six, and the gull seven. No runtime asset needs
colour quantization by the integration worker.

## Intended animation and layering

- **Water:** repeat 256 px horizontally; it also tiles vertically at 128 px. Both pairs of opposing
  edge rows/columns match exactly. Scroll by one logical pixel every 100 ms (a 25.6 s horizontal loop).
  This is one scrollable texture, not independently generated wave frames. Freeze the offset for still
  mode. Keep the sea dark: this set has one universal water colourway, not six time-specific seas.
- **Foam:** repeat horizontally; optionally scroll at one pixel per 160 ms. Clip/place it along the
  visible hull-water contact, below the hull and above the water. The strip is generic and is **not**
  pre-fitted to hull C's curved silhouette. Its top/bottom are transparent; do not repeat vertically.
- **Sky:** draw one complete opaque phase, then the next over it with opacity `t` in `[0,1]`. Do not
  fade both over transparency. All six use exactly the same pixel shapes, so clouds do not jump during
  a fade. They are full backdrops, **not tileable textures**. Keep their destination rectangles aligned;
  crop or extend the viewport treatment during integration rather than repeating cloud edges.
- **Clock:** the manifest offers local wall-clock phase anchors at 05:30, 08:00, 12:00, 16:00, 18:30,
  and 21:00. Interpolate cyclically between adjacent anchors, including night through midnight to dawn.
  These are illustrative defaults, not astronomical sunrise/sunset. An integration using location and
  season can assign the same layers to calculated sunrise, solar noon and sunset. No clock logic is
  included here. Intermediate colours come from blending; no extra transition PNGs are necessary.
- **Stars:** fade independently with night weight, above the sky and behind the ship; no moon is baked
  in. **Gull:** use `up, mid, down, mid` at 125 ms per pose (500 ms loop), or hold `mid` for a distant
  glide. Anchor all frames at `[26,20]` (beak registration); mirror the whole sprite to fly left. Its
  body and beak align, but the wing sweep is a generated approximation, not anatomical animation.

## Provenance and cleanup

Created on 2026-10-14 with the built-in `image_gen__imagegen` tool in **10 calls**, below the 25-call cap.
The tool does not disclose a backend model; no specific Imagen model is claimed. Source artwork was
generated, not drawn by a procedural renderer. References were this repository's approved hull C atlas
and the local approved art. No external artist's image or third-party texture was used.

`environment-provenance.json` records every exact prompt and source SHA-256. Original generator PNGs
are retained on the generation host in `environment-source/`, beside the fresh checkout, as with the
existing deck's uncommitted source images. The mechanical cleanup recipe and crop coordinates are in
the manifest. There is no build-time dependency on those source files.

Cleanup used block-median sampling, hard alpha thresholds, palette mapping and edge colour matching.
Sky variants were independently generated by editing the midday source. After quantization and a 3 x 3
median denoise of midday, each variant's median colour per midday colour region was transferred to
that common pixel geometry. This removes gradient noise and guarantees registered cross-fades; it
does not invent cloud shapes. The gull poses were cropped at one fixed scale and registered by beak.
Stars' generated glows were removed. No asset artwork was hand-painted or procedurally drawn; review
previews mechanically composite the generated assets with the approved hull.

## Honest quality review

The sea is usable at the deck's scale, with quiet wavelets and verified seams. Its repeated glints become
recognizable over a large expanse; a human artist could make a less repetitive second tile. Scrolling
provides drift, not wave deformation. The foam is the weakest part: its scallops are too regular and can
look like a rope when isolated. A human artist should break up the rhythm and fit it to hull C.

The sky set is usable as a deliberately restrained banded backdrop. The shared geometry makes blends
coherent; morning and afternoon are intentionally close. It is not a detailed cloud animation. The
ship itself stays day-lit in the preview: lighting/tinting the hull and sea is integration work.

The gull reads clearly at scale. The three source poses have slight shape variation, and the middle
pose is an angled wing rather than a truly flat glide. The seven-colour cleanup and fixed registration
help, but a human artist should refine the wing sweep for polished animation.

`environment-preview.png` shows all phases with the existing hull (no foam fitting or runtime lighting).
`environment-detail.png` shows a 2 x 2 water repeat, foam and enlarged poses. `gull/flap-preview.gif`
is an enlarged animation preview, not a runtime sprite. `environment-verification.json` records the
artifact checks. These are asset-level checks and visual composites, not plugin integration tests.
